Pin 01 · Uptown, Oakland

Tanuki Izakaya

The kitchen finally stopped apologising. Eighteen months ago the menu had a burger on it. What replaced that is a counter, a charcoal grill and a card you can read in one breath.

The binchotan runs hot from five and the skewers come in the order they finish rather than the order you asked for them, which is the correct way and still surprises people. The chicken is broken down in-house; you can taste it in the neck and the tail.

Must order: the tsukune, twice. Skip: the sashimi board — twenty-eight dollars with nothing to do with the rest of the meal.
