# Round 1 feedback — how seven concepts became three

Maria reviewed all seven concepts and recorded a voice memo per concept.
This is the distillation and what was done about it. The raw recordings and
transcripts are not in the repo; they are personal and are kept outside it.

---

## The verdict

**Concept D is the direction.** Her words: *"my obvious favourite… it's
definitely closest to what I have in mind, so I would use this as a basis."*
Concept G was vetoed outright.

The other five all failed the same way and passed the same way: she rejected
the **type and colour** and kept the **structure**. On concept F: *"if I was
seeing this in the colour and in the font of concept D then I'd probably have
some different opinions."* That sentence is the whole reorganisation — the
question was never which of seven designs wins, it was which structures
survive once they are all wearing D's skin.

So round 2 is not three competing designs. It is one design across three
pages.

## Concept by concept

| **G — Plain** | "Vetoed" | Being centred |

## Changes made to D

1. **Palette pinned to her four.** Baby blue, navy, cream, and — new — a dark
   grey for body text, which D did not have (it set body copy in navy). Navy
   is now reserved for headings, rules and structure.
2. **Whimsier wordmark.** "I think that Maria Eleni could be more whimsical."
   The script moved from Italianno to Parisienne — rounder, warmer, less
   copperplate.
3. **A real bow.** "The bow could be a little bit thicker and sleeker, like a
   big bow that you would put in your hair for a celebration." It was line
   art; it is now a filled ribbon, larger, with a darker knot.
4. **Search, promoted.** Her one explicit gap: *"I want people to immediately
   find a search bar."* It now sits in the masthead on all three pages, with
   example queries under it on the homepage.
5. **Rubric and article moved off the homepage** to page 3, where they belong.
   The homepage keeps a pointer. The three pages no longer repeat each other.

## The three pages

- **`1-home.html`** — D refined, plus C's standing columns and a B-style
  rankings module sized as a doorway to page 2.
- **`2-map.html`** — F's structure in D's skin, with B's ledger sitting
  directly under the map, which is where she put it.
- **`3-review.html`** — A's article structure with D's seven-axis rubric, plus
  C's rubric explainer. Pull quotes, the short version, dish photographs with
  descriptions, and the address block with a map.

## Still open

1. **The display face.** D leads with Cormorant Garamond, a serif, while six
   of her seven reference sites are sans. She said she liked D's font better
   than the others, so it stays for now — but a sans variant would test
   whether the script wordmark alone is carrying the personality.
2. **Composite score versus axes only.** Vegan-friendliness and supply
   transparency do not average cleanly against food quality. The current
   answer is an unweighted mean with the reasoning spelled out on the page;
   the alternative is to publish the seven numbers and no composite.
3. **Where the map lives in the nav.** It is currently a top-level item
   ("Map & lists") alongside Food, Wine and About. She has not said whether
   it should sit there or under Food.
4. **Whether the ledger needs facets at all.** The archive is small, and two
   of her favourite reference sites ship indexes with zero filtering.
